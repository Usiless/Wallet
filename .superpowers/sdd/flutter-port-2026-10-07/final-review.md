## P1 — Effective obligation payments lose their three-day Wallet duplicate protection

Location: `lib/core/notifications/notification_ingestion_service.dart:202` and `:380` (both scheduled-ledger predicates). Producer: `lib/features/payments/domain/pending_payment_service.dart:333–345` and `lib/features/templates/scheduled_template_service.dart:253–269`.

Concrete trigger: a template prepares an obligation for 100 with a recognized merchant and bank account. The user confirms the entire 100, then a normal recognized Wallet notification for the same account/merchant/amount is posted one hour later (within the approved three-day scheduled-purchase window). This is a plausible difference because the payment UI records a date independently of the bank notification time.

The confirmed payment has `source=manual` (TransactionDraft's default), `externalFingerprint=pending:<id>:<revision>`, and `sourceReference=scheduled:<template>:<date>`. Neither scheduled-ledger predicate recognizes that sourceReference: they only recognize recurring source or scheduled external fingerprint. The obligation has remainingMinor zero and is excluded from both open-obligation guards; the ordinary ledger match covers only two minutes. Therefore process reaches automatic confirmation, and its transactional guard also allows a second expense/debit. The nominal-or-residual repair does not cover this completed-payment transition. This breaks the preserved baseline rule that a Wallet match following a scheduled record requires review.

The same producer and predicates also confirm the non-template variant: a noncredit installment paid in full carries the same `pending:` external fingerprint (sourceReference is its plan ID), and a matching Wallet notification one hour later creates a second expense. A partial actual payment of40 against nominal100/residual60 followed by a Wallet40 also bypasses both amount guards. These are the same missing recognition of effective linked payments, not separate speculative findings. Actual payment account/amount/date must be read from the ledger rather than inferred from the original obligation.

Requested bounded correction: recognize effective linked obligation payments (`pending:` external fingerprint) and preserve scheduled-origin recognition in both three-day ledger guards, retaining the same actual account, merchant, amount and active-status checks. Add real-service regressions for process and stale automatic confirm after scheduled and non-template obligations are paid, including a matched actual partial payment; assert the candidate stays pending and that only the original payment/posting exists. Retain an unrelated ordinary Wallet control and the ordinary manual two-minute policy; do not broaden all manual expenses to three days. This finding is confirmed by source/control-flow inspection; this read-only reviewer did not run a reproducer.
